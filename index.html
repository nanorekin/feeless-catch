All the rules

Playing

Move your wallet by dragging on the field, or with the arrow keys (or A and D). Catch the good things and dodge the red fee blocks. Press P or Esc, or the pause button, to pause.

Lives

You start with 3 lives. Catching a fee costs one life. Hearts give one back, up to 3. The run ends when you reach 0.

Points, streak and multiplier

A coin is worth 1 point and a gold coin 5. Your streak counts the catches in a row since you last caught a fee. Letting a coin go does not break it. Every 6 catches in the streak adds 1 to your multiplier, up to x5.

Frenzy

Every 20 catches in a row starts a 5-second frenzy where all points double.

Levels

You level up every 9 coins you catch. Each level makes things fall faster and more often, and brings more fee blocks. From level 3, some fee blocks drift sideways and items sometimes fall in pairs. The scenery changes at levels 3, 5, 7 and 9.

Power-ups

From level 2, a power-up badge sometimes falls. Catch it to switch it on. Only one badge falls at a time, and the key above shows what each one does and how long it lasts. The shield lasts until it blocks one fee, or until its time runs out. Big and mini wallet cancel each other out, and the mini wallet is the rarest. Your streak multiplier, the mini wallet and a frenzy all multiply together. From level 3, a rare broccoli also falls. Catch it and every fee on screen turns into a coin for 5 seconds, and new fees turn into coins too. It never stacks with a badge already on screen, and it can't turn a fee you've already caught back into a life.

Extra life

When a run ends, you can send 0.05 XNO for one extra life. You carry on with 1 life and keep your score, but your streak and power-ups reset. It works once per run.
